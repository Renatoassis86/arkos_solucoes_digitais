# ARKOS Capability Map

## Digital Experience
Corporate and institutional sites, landing ecosystems, portals, e-commerce, multilingual experiences, content platforms, design systems, accessibility, SEO, CRO and analytics.

## Software & Platforms
Custom web applications, SaaS, client/partner portals, intranets/extranets, dashboards, workflow systems, APIs, integrations, modernization and vertical platforms.

## AI & Automation
AI agents, copilots, RAG/knowledge systems, document intelligence, workflow automation, classification/extraction, recommendation/prediction, conversational interfaces and AI governance.

## Data & Intelligence
Data engineering, warehouses/lakes, BI, product analytics, marketing/sales analytics, experimentation, forecasting, scoring and decision-support systems.

## Technology Advisory
CTO as a Service, architecture, technical due diligence, cloud/DevOps, security/privacy, observability, FinOps, platform strategy, engineering governance and squads.

## Growth Technology
SEO, technical content systems, marketing automation, CRM/revenue integrations, attribution, conversion optimization, experimentation and MarTech architecture.

## Commercial packaging principle
Package around business outcomes and maturity stages rather than only hours or technologies. Use discovery/diagnostic offers to reduce uncertainty, implementation offers to create the asset, and recurring optimization/support offers to generate continuity and measurable improvement.
